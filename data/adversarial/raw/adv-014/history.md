From 1 June, parcels for HV postcodes can be upgraded to Express Air from Port Ardrin depot for next-morning delivery. Book through Scheduling before 12:00 for the next morning.

The following are dangerous goods by air and cannot go on Express Air in any quantity: perfume, eau de toilette and aftershave; aerosols of any kind (deodorant, hairspray, body mist, dry shampoo); nail varnish and remover; lighters and refills; loose lithium batteries and power banks. Parcels containing any of these stay on the road and ferry service.
