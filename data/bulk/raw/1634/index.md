## Context

A consignment of personal household effects sent by sea arrived months later than expected. The consignee reported damage on delivery and the desk asked for a claim to be registered.

## Initial request

I don't have much left in me for this. Eight months. I was told 'on schedule' in March and 'progressing well' in May and it turns out it sat on a dock until June. The tracker never worked, not once. My mother's china is in pieces, and when I told Nettlebank Arc, nobody replied at all. I have no receipts. These are things I have owned for thirty years. On Friday I sent the whole file to the Freight Ombudsman, complaint reference FO-26-1187. They have asked for a written statement from your desk of what happened and when, and whether a claim exists. Please send that. I don't want another message telling me everything is fine.
