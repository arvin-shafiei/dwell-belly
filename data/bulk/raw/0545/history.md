Two invoices are open on that account: IB-3196 against DL-2699 and IB-7201 against DL-2987. Each carries a line 'Clearance handling fee'. That is Whinmoor Arc's own service charge for presenting the entry to customs. It is not duty or tax, and customs neither sets nor receives it, so please do not describe it as a customs charge. It appears in the fee schedule of the emailed quote. The fee can be reviewed, invoice by invoice, where the customer prepared the entry documents himself. To open a review we need two things: the number of the invoice he is disputing, or both, and a copy of the customs clearance notice for that consignment showing who is named as declarant. We do not hold the notice when a customer self-files. No review is open and we cannot say what the outcome would be.
