## Initial request

Ms Quill, the eleven boxes were left at the bottom of my steps, in the open, at ten to one. I asked a civil question at booking and was told the steps would 'delay the round'. I asked again through you and received an email from Dispatch informing me that I was 'asking staff to put themselves at risk'. I am a sixty-eight year old woman eleven weeks out from a hip replacement, who asked whether two men could carry boxes up six steps and offered to pay for it. I learned from the driver, who was perfectly pleasant, that there is a paid service for exactly this which nobody mentioned. Having no alternative I carried the two lightest boxes up myself this afternoon. I felt something go in my hip on the second, and I am now sitting with the leg raised waiting for my surgeon's office to call back. Nine boxes are still outside and rain is forecast tonight. I regard the email of the 21st as discriminatory and I intend to make a formal complaint on that basis. I should like the name of the person who wrote it. I should also like to know what you propose to do.
