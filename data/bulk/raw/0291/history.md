I dispute how I came to be charged for it. Your booking form asked me for the weight and I gave it. It asked for the address and your own system marked it residential. The option was an unticked box beneath a block of small print, below the button most people would press. It did not warn me, it did not stop me, and it did not tell me a charge would follow. Then the charge was taken from a stored card, afterwards, without notice. That is not an oversight on my part. It is a form designed so that the customer misses the box and pays later. And I understand your billing team cannot even produce what I was shown. So: reverse the 68.00 and confirm to me in writing by Friday that it has been done. If not I shall dispute it with my card provider and write up, in detail, how this portal works.
