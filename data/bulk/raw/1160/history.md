## 2028-03-09 11:11:00 UTC — event 007

- **Stage:** Context
- **Channel:** Email
- **From:** Niamh Eckersall, solicitor, Marrick & Stowe
- **To:** Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

LETTER BEFORE ACTION. Our client: Ms Rosalind Cresswell. Your ref: DL-1430.

We act for Ms Cresswell in respect of damage caused to her property on 28 February 2028 during a delivery coordinated by the Rivet Delivery Desk and carried out by Emberlee Arc Logistics. We propose to name both as defendants.

By your portal message of 3 March you acknowledged the damage and advised our client that her claim would be dealt with by the carrier. Six days have passed without contact. We treat that message as an acknowledgement of responsibility.

We require within 14 days of this letter: (1) a written admission of liability; (2) payment of GBP 1,840.00 for the floor and GBP 310.00 costs to date, total GBP 2,150.00. Failing that we are instructed to issue proceedings without further notice.

You must preserve all messages, call records and logs relating to DL-1430. Please confirm by return the name of the desk's insurer. All further communication is to come to this office. Do not contact our client directly.
