## Context

A consignee bought two pallets of flooring from a supplier and booked Saltmarrow Arc freight to collect them, a job carried out by a subcontractor. The collection was marked failed. Collections control owns the collection record; Freight planner deals with the subcontractor.

## Initial request

Five weeks. Several thousand pounds of oak. Your driver says someone else took it, the supplier says you took it and now has a signed note to prove it, and your planner's contribution is that it 'isn't in the network', as though that were an answer. Somebody drove out of that yard with my floor. Either Saltmarrow Arc has it and has lost it, or someone has taken it using Saltmarrow Arc's name, and either way that is fraud and I want it treated as fraud. I have been told three times that 'we have emailed the subcontractor'. I have fitters booked and a client who has stopped returning my calls. Do not tell me it was never collected so there is nothing to look for.
