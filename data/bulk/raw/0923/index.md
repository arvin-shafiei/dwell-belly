## Context

A business account that ships textiles abroad with Heronwick Arc has been invoiced for import duty and tax on three export shipments. Import billing at Heronwick Arc owns those invoices; Business billing and the desk cannot open or amend them.

## Initial request

Eleven years and roughly a van a day with Heronwick Arc, and I am explaining who pays import duty to a helpdesk. It is written on the invoice. In English. It has been written on every invoice we have ever sent abroad. I am not interested in another 'we have passed it on'. What I want from you, in writing, today, is confirmation that all three invoices were raised in error and that credits totalling GBP 1,286.40 will be on the September statement. If I do not have that by close of business the August account goes unpaid and the contract goes out to tender. I imagine somebody there can do the sum on what that costs you.
