DL-6010: destination customs raised import VAT and duty on entry on 17 February. The returned-goods declaration was not applied to the assessment; we don't know why. The consignee, Sedgemoor's returns centre, declined to pay on 19 February and refused the parcel. It is in return transit and due back with the sender around 5 March. We cannot cancel or amend a customs assessment. Only the importer of record, which is the consignee, can dispute it and claim returned-goods relief. Nothing has been billed to the sender; the unpaid charges are parked against the shipment.
