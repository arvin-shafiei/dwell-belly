# Case 1311: Six-carton household move held at the export hub with one carton unaccounted for

- **Escalated:** 2028-10-13 09:50:00 UTC

- **Data:** Entirely synthetic; fictional geography; all times UTC

## Context

DL-4451 is an international consignment of six cartons of personal effects moving under a single customs declaration. Five cartons reached the Aldermoor export hub; carton 4 did not. The recipient account and shipment match are verified.

## Initial request

I have used Oxenhale Arc for every move I have made in twenty years and I have never had cause to write a message like this one. I do not want the money. Nothing in carton 4 could be bought again at any price: my mother's letters, my late husband's sketchbooks, the albums. What I would like is to understand what 'not located' means. Whether somebody has stood in a room and looked along the shelves for a brown box with green tape, or whether a screen was checked and that was that. I suspect the second, and I would rather be told so than be sent another offer. And the other five boxes, which are only my clothes and my kitchen, have now sat at the hub for three and a half weeks on account of the one. May they travel without it? I would be grateful to be told honestly, either way.
