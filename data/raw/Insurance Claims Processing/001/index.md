## Initial request

I’d like a buildings insurance quotation for my flat at 101 Birch Walk, Brackenford, ZZ1 1ZZ, which I’m planning to let. Please let me know what information you need to obtain a quote.

## Overview

Macy Harper asked Erin Cole of Alderbrook Cover for a quotation for her flat to be let. Erin requested the planned letting start, use and property details; Macy supplied them, and Erin submitted the completed information to Northmere Mutual. The insurer confirmed that it had accepted the quotation request, but had not provided a price or issued a policy.

## Next action at escalation

Erin’s escalation was to obtain Macy’s planned letting start and the missing use and property details, then submit the completed information to Northmere Mutual for a quotation. The next step is to await the insurer’s pricing response and relay it to Macy.

## Outcome

Northmere Mutual accepted the request for a quotation; no price or policy has been provided.
