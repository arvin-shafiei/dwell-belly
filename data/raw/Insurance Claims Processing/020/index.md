- **Insurer:** Northmere Insurance

## Initial request

I’ve sold my home at 120 Beech Walk, Brackenford, ZZ3 2ZZ, and would like my property policy stopped. Please let me know what you need from me to deal with this.

## Overview

Ethan told Meg Rowan at Fairholt Cover that he had sold the insured home and wanted the policy stopped, but initially gave no completion date. Meg asked him to confirm the date and his requested action; after Ethan replied, Meg sent the request to Northmere Insurance. The insurer reviewed the sale and cancellation request but declined to backdate cancellation to 18 May on the information supplied. It confirmed the policy remains active and asked Ethan for a prospective cancellation date; Meg relayed this and made clear no premium adjustment had been agreed.

## Next action at escalation

At the escalation point, Meg needed to obtain Ethan’s completion date and then send his request to stop the policy to Northmere Insurance.

## Outcome

Northmere Insurance reviewed the cancellation request and confirmed that the policy remains active, not cancelled on 18 May; Meg informed Ethan of the status and the need to confirm a prospective date if he still wishes to cancel.
