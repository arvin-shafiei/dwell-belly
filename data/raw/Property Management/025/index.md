## Initial request

The oven has stopped heating up. Does not work on any settings, the light still switches on and it makes a sound but does not heat up on oven or grill settings. It was replaced sometime last year as the last one stopped working too.

## Overview

The tenant reported that the oven would not heat on any setting, although its light and sound still worked, and noted that it had been replaced the previous year. The landlord asked Northbridge Lettings to contact the contractor that had replaced it, seeking a charge-free warranty repair. Harbour Services initially reported that the oven was just outside its 12-month warranty; after further investigation, it was found to fall within a two-year manufacturer's warranty. The manufacturer’s engineer attended, parts were ordered, and the provider later confirmed the repair was completed under warranty. The record contains inconsistent appointment dates and descriptions, including references to 21 April, 14 May, 21 May and 25 May; these are retained as recorded rather than reconciled.

## Outcome

The contractor confirmed that the oven repair was completed under warranty, with no charge to the landlord. The task was marked complete without payment.
