## Initial request

The toilet is running slow to fill up and the cold water tap in the hand basin in the bathroom isn't working at all this has become a issue recently on our return from our holiday.

Additional details provided:
- Is this the only toilet at the property? Yes
- Is the toilet not flushing, blocked and overflowing, constantly running or trickling, or something else? Trickling to fill up
- When you flush, does the water drain away at all, or does it rise up toward the rim of the bowl? Drains away
- Are there any access considerations we should know about, such as entry codes or alarms and security systems that would need to be disarmed? No
- Are there any pets at the property? Yes

## Overview

The tenants reported that the toilet was trickling as it refilled and the cold bathroom basin tap was not working after their holiday. The landlord approved proceeding, and a plumbing provider was assigned. After repeated reminders, the provider arranged an appointment; the tenants confirmed someone would be home. The provider missed the confirmed visit, then reported completing the work the following day: a new Fluidmaster bottom-entry ball valve was supplied and installed in the toilet, and the tenants said the basin taps were fine. The landlord paid the contractor directly; the task was completed without payment through the case.

## Outcome

The provider replaced the toilet cistern inlet valve. The tenants reported that the basin taps were working normally.
