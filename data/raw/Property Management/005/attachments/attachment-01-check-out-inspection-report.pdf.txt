Northbridge Lettings

Check-out inspection report
Records the condition, cleanliness, meter information, defects and photographic evidence for the
check-out inspection of Flat 10, 160 Alder Lane, Westmarsh, ZZ8 9ZZ.


Appointment date: 25 February 2025. Assessor: Northbridge Lettings.

The report provides a visual, room-by-room schedule of condition and contents. Where the
assessor makes no comment about an element or item, it is treated as being in good, clean,
serviceable condition and without defects. Observed defects and poor condition are described and
supported by photographs.

The report is not a structural survey or repair-cost assessment. Fixtures and fittings are listed but
may not have been tested. Inaccessible and loft areas are not inspected; locked rooms and
outbuildings containing landlord belongings are not inspected. The report remains the property of
the instructing party and is not to be used without written permission.

End-of-tenancy guidance: the property and its contents are expected to be left in a similar
condition and state of cleanliness to the pre-tenancy report, allowing for fair wear and tear. Repair,
replacement or professional cleaning costs may be deducted from the tenant's deposit; receipts
should be retained where work is carried out. Cleaning should be thorough and the property left
tidy. Carpets and curtains should be professionally cleaned where required by the inventory or
tenancy agreement; vinyl or tile flooring should be clean and free of cracks or tears. Decor should
be left in the same state as at the start of the tenancy; hooks, nails, screws and holes may need to
be removed or repaired, and excessive marks and fingerprints removed. Refrigerators and
freezers should be defrosted, switched off and left open. Ovens, hobs, extractor units and
microwaves should be cleaned, including burnt-on deposits, grease, doors and seals. Applicable
gardens should be left neat and tidy. Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms were stated to have
been checked and working as of the report date; tenants are responsible for keeping them in
working order and reporting faults other than battery replacement.

The report's condition ratings range from Excellent (unused or new) through Good, Fair and Poor
to Unacceptable (not fit for purpose in its present state). Cleanliness ratings range from Excellent
(cleaned to a high standard) through Good and Fair to Poor (light cleaning required) and
Unacceptable (professional cleaning required).

Findings
    Entrance/Hallway — Condition Fair; cleanliness Poor. Front door, ceiling, lighting, walls and
    cupboard were recorded as per inventory. Cleaning was required; the summary notes a light
    clean. The overhead door closer was damaged and requires repair.
    Bedroom 1 — Condition Poor; cleanliness Unacceptable. Door, ceiling and lighting were
    recorded as per inventory with no issues. Walls had very minor marks considered consistent
    with fair wear and tear. Balcony was recorded as per inventory with no issues; blinds and
    radiator were recorded as per inventory. Flooring/carpet was dirty and required vacuum
    cleaning. Chest of drawers was missing. Wardrobe was recorded as per inventory with no
    issues. Balcony doors did not open and were recorded as stuck.
    Bedroom 1 en-suite — Condition Fair; cleanliness Poor. Door, ceiling, radiator, flooring and
    toilet were recorded as per inventory with no issues, except the walls showed a peeling area.
    Basin and shower required cleaning. The extractor fan was non-functional and did not
    activate.
    Living Room — Condition Poor; cleanliness Fair. Laminate flooring was dusty and required
    cleaning. Door, ceiling, balcony, balcony windows and radiator were recorded as per
    inventory with no issues, except blinds had bent slats not mentioned in the inventory. A
    coffee stain was recorded on the walls.
    Kitchen — Condition Poor; cleanliness Unacceptable. Walls and tiled splashback were
    recorded as per inventory. Cupboard fronts and internal cabinets were clean; the oven was
    clean. The hob and metal splashback were very dirty, worktops were dirty, and the sink and
    taps were filthy and required professional cleaning. Washing machine and fridge were in fair
    condition. Freezer required defrosting and cleaning. Melamine kitchen cabinets were
    peeling.
    Bathroom — Condition Poor; cleanliness Unacceptable. Door, ceiling and lighting were
    recorded as per inventory. Flooring, sink, bath, shower enclosure and equipment, toilet, dark
    grey painted walls and mirror were dirty and required professional cleaning. Wallpaper walls
    were in poor condition. The area above the toilet was in very poor condition and required
    renewal.
    Bedroom 2 — Condition Unacceptable; cleanliness Unacceptable. Door, ceiling, lighting,
    window and blinds were recorded as per inventory with no issues. White-painted walls had
    gouges not mentioned in the inventory. Radiator issues were noted. Flooring was in very
    poor condition and required professional cleaning; the carpet required cleaning.

Conclusions
    The report records a broad requirement for cleaning, with Unacceptable cleanliness ratings
    in bedroom 1, the kitchen, bathroom and bedroom 2.
    Reported maintenance or condition matters include a damaged hallway overhead closer,
    stuck balcony doors in bedroom 1, a non-functioning en-suite extractor fan, peeling en-suite
    paint, a coffee stain on living-room walls, peeling kitchen cupboards, gouged bedroom 2
    walls and radiator issues in bedroom 2.
    The report is an inspection record and does not state that any cleaning or repairs were
    subsequently completed.
