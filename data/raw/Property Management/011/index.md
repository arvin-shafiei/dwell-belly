## Overview

The tenant reported that the dishwasher had recently started running without completing its cycle. The landlord approved an inspection and later authorised the quoted repair. The provider found water in the base and a loose flow-through heater connection, removed the water, tightened the connection, and tested the appliance; it was then running as it should. The provider also reported that a dial was missing and quoted £95 including parts, labour and VAT for its replacement. The tenant was told the replacement knob would be installed by them. The record says the request was closed after final checks.

## Outcome

The provider removed water from the dishwasher base, tightened a loose connection and tested the appliance, which was running as it should. A replacement dial was identified as needed and quoted at £95 including parts, labour and VAT; the tenant was told they would install the replacement knob themselves. The request was marked closed after final checks.
