## Initial request

Hi Clara,

The tenants have advised that the key snapped for the back door for the conservatory and they do not have a spare.

Please can you replace the lock and provide 4 new keys, 2 for the tenants, 1 for us and 1 for the landlord. The ones for us and the landlord will need to be given to the office.

## Overview

The tenant reported that the key for the conservatory back door had snapped and that there was no spare. The landlord approved the work and requested new keys for the tenants, landlord and property management. Northbridge Lettings assigned Alder Compliance. The provider asked for the tenant’s contact details to arrange the appointment; the agency supplied them and later sent a reminder. The provider reported that the job was completed, the cylinder and handles had been replaced, and the remaining keys would be dropped at the office the following day. No quote was required because the repair was completed on site. The request was closed after the final checks.

## Outcome

The provider reported replacing the cylinder and handles and said the remaining keys would be delivered to the office the following day. The request was marked completed and closed.
