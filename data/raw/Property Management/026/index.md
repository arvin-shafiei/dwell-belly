## Overview

The tenant reported that the main oven would not heat, although the grill worked. The landlord approved a repair, and Keystone Compliance was assigned. The appointment date was clarified with the tenant after a scheduling discrepancy. On the visit, the heating element was replaced and the hobs were cleaned. The tenant later confirmed the oven was working properly and was satisfied. The request was closed.

## Outcome

The oven’s heating element was replaced. The tenant confirmed by phone that the oven was working normally afterward; the technician also cleaned the hobs.
