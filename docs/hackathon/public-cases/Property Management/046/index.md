## Initial request

Bedroom window is not closing  properly one of the hinges are rusted

## Overview

The tenant reported that a bedroom window would not close properly because one hinge was rusted. The landlord approved a contractor visit and later approved the quoted hinge replacement cost of £90 plus VAT. The contractor arranged an appointment, and the agency later followed up for confirmation of the work and an invoice. The agency informed the landlord that the window issue was resolved and sent the invoice. The request was closed after the agency completed its checks.

## Outcome

Northbridge Lettings confirmed to the landlord that the window issue had been resolved and forwarded the contractor's invoice. The request was subsequently closed.
