## Initial request

Request summary with context: The Willow Maintenance washing machine is experiencing a power failure and is not operational.

Tenant's original query: Washing machine broken, it’s LG , we have googled it, it has power failure and can’t be fixed, I am willing to replace, this one was here when I moved in, but if I replace it will be mine and not become part of the property, if I ever was to move out I would take it with me.

## Overview

The tenant reported that the washing machine had stopped working due to a power failure and offered to replace it with her own machine, which she would take when she moved out. The agency contacted the landlord, who approved the replacement and agreed to cover removal costs if charged. The tenant then said her brother would take the old machine to the tip. The request was completed without payment.

## Outcome

The landlord approved the tenant installing her own washing machine and taking it with her when she vacates. The tenant arranged for her brother to take the old machine to the tip.
