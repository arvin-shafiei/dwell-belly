## Initial request

Front door you have to slam to shut like the back. when fridge man arrived he even commented and said it will cause a shatter in glass- he said he was also going to report to you.

## Overview

The tenant reported that the front door was increasingly difficult to close and had to be slammed; the contractor also reported that the door and frame wobbled. The new fridge freezer had been fitted. The agency contacted the landlord, who suggested using the key to open the latch before closing the door and said a contractor could look at it next month if needed. The tenant tried the key method, found it worked, and agreed to monitor the door and report any recurrence. The request was completed without payment.

## Outcome

The tenant tried opening the latch with the key before closing the door, and it worked. She will continue using this method, monitor the issue and let the agency know if it recurs. The request was closed without payment.
