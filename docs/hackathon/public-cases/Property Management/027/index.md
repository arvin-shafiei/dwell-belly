## Overview

The tenant reported that the bath was draining slowly after showers. Northbridge Lettings advised trying a drain cleaner and plunger, explaining that internal drain blockages caused by everyday use were the tenant’s responsibility and that a contractor visit could be chargeable. After follow-up messages, the tenant said a plunger had cleared the drain. The task was recorded as resolved without a contractor and completed without payment. Later correspondence in the same record concerned the tenant’s separate question about registering for a garage parking space; block management had been contacted, with further information to follow.

## Outcome

The tenant confirmed that they had cleared the bath blockage themselves using a plunger. The request was completed without a contractor or payment.
