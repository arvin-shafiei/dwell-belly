## Initial request

Hey, the washing machine isn't spinning when it comes to the end of a cycle with washing inside the washer. No matter the cycle, with clothes in it doesn't spin/drain resulting in powering off the machine and draining it mainly front the front drain pipe. The cycle says at "0:01" minute left indefinitely as it continues to spin, presumably to try and remove the water.

I have cleaned/empited out the front filter and fully drained the machine twice since it started. Both times because the door wouldn't unlock due to water being left in the machine. 

Thanks,
Turner

## Overview

The tenant reported that the washing machine would not spin or drain properly and that they had cleaned the filter and drained it twice. Northbridge Lettings asked the landlord to approve an appliance engineer visit, with a stated minimum cost of £65.00 plus VAT. The landlord first suggested a possible blocked pump and later arranged for an operative to attend. The operative could not fix the machine, so the landlord contacted the repair company that had fitted it. The landlord subsequently confirmed that the washing machine had been fixed. A separate towel-rail repair remained in progress, with a replacement part ordered.

## Outcome

The landlord confirmed that the repair company had fixed the washing machine. The request was marked completed without payment.
