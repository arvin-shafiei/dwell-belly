## Initial request

I own the property at 128 Hazel Walk, Brackenford, ZZ4 1ZZ, and I can see two £286.40 debits on 18 and 20 June 2025. Could you check what each payment was for? I have not been able to tell whether they are duplicates.

## Overview

Lydia reported two debits of the same amount to Pat at Hawthorn Risk. Pat recorded the transaction details, including a masked payment reference, and asked Crownmere Insurance's financial team to reconcile them. The team matched the 18 June debit to June's instalment and the 20 June debit to July's instalment collected in advance. Pat passed that result to Lydia, who confirmed she had received it.

## Next action at escalation

Send the two transaction amounts, transaction dates (18 and 20 June 2025), and masked reference to Crownmere Insurance's financial team for reconciliation, without promising a refund.

## Outcome

Crownmere Insurance's financial team confirmed that the 20 June £286.40 debit was July's instalment collected in advance rather than a second June payment, and Pat communicated this result to Lydia.
