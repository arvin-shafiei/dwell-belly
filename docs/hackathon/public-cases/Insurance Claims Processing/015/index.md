## Initial request

I’m writing to let you know that the tenancy at 115 Willow Walk, Brackenford, ZZ2 6ZZ has ended. The flat is no longer being let, and I’d like to know what I need to do about my insurance.

## Overview

Grace Ford told her broker, Matt at Pinehaven Risk, that the tenancy at 115 Willow Walk, Brackenford, ZZ2 6ZZ had ended, without giving the end date or explaining how the flat was being used. Matt asked for those details, then notified Northmere Mutual after Grace replied that she had moved into the flat. Northmere Mutual confirmed receipt of the update and outlined further steps, which Matt relayed to Grace.

## Next action at escalation

At the clarification stage, Matt needed to establish the tenancy end date and the flat’s current use, then notify Northmere Mutual.

## Outcome

Northmere Mutual confirmed receipt of the information and advised further steps; it did not confirm that the policy had been changed or cancelled.
